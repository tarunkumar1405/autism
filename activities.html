Practical Activities

Simple activities that can be included naturally in everyday routines.

🍪

Give Me Game

Use a favourite item. Pause and give your child an opportunity to request it using speech, gesture, picture or AAC.

👋

Copy Me

Copy simple actions such as clapping, tapping or waving and encourage your child to copy you.

🧹

Everyday Helper

Give your child simple household jobs such as putting toys away or carrying clothes.

🧸

Turn Taking

Roll a ball, take turns stacking blocks or play a simple game where each person has a turn.

📚

Picture Book

Look at pictures together and talk about objects, people and actions.

🎵

Action Songs

Use simple songs with movements to encourage imitation, attention and participation.
