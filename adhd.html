Understanding ADHD

Learn about attention, impulsivity, activity levels and practical support strategies.

What is ADHD?

ADHD is a neurodevelopmental condition that can affect attention, impulse control and activity level.

🎯

Attention

A child may find it difficult to stay focused on tasks that are not highly interesting.

⚡

Activity Level

Some children may have a strong need for movement and activity.

🧠

Impulsivity

A child may act quickly before thinking about what will happen next.

Practical Support

🎯 Short Tasks

Break larger tasks into smaller, manageable steps.

⏰ Routines

Predictable routines can make everyday activities easier.

⭐ Positive Feedback

Notice and praise useful behaviours and effort.
