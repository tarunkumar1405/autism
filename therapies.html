Therapies Explained

Learn what different professionals may focus on and what questions parents can ask.

🗣️

Speech Therapy

May focus on communication, understanding language, speech and alternative communication.

👐

Occupational Therapy

May support daily living skills, participation, motor skills and sensory needs.

🧩

Behavioural Support

May focus on understanding behaviour and teaching useful skills.

📚

Special Education

Can provide individualized educational and developmental support.

📱

AAC

Augmentative and Alternative Communication can help children communicate using pictures, devices, signs or other systems.

👨‍👩‍👦

Parent-Mediated Support

Parents learn strategies that can be used naturally during everyday routines.

Questions Parents Can Ask

  • What goals will we work on?
  • How will progress be measured?
  • What can we practise at home?
  • How often should progress be reviewed?

Remember

A therapy plan should be individualized to the child's needs and regularly reviewed with qualified professionals.
