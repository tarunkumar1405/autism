About Growing Together

Growing Together is an educational resource for parents, caregivers and families who want practical, respectful and easy-to-understand information about supporting children with autism, ADHD and related developmental needs.

Our approach focuses on communication, learning, participation, daily living skills, social development and independence. We believe every child develops differently and that progress can take many forms.

Our approach

  • Practical ideas that families can try at home.
  • Clear explanations of common topics and therapies.
  • Respectful, child-centred language.
  • No promises of a cure or guaranteed outcomes.
  • Encouragement to work with qualified professionals when appropriate.
Important: Growing Together is an educational information website. It is not a substitute for an assessment, diagnosis or individualized advice from a qualified healthcare, therapy or education professional.
