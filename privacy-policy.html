How Growing Together handles information and advertising

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Growing Together ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our website.

Information we collect

Growing Together is primarily an informational website. We do not intentionally ask visitors to provide sensitive personal information simply to read our content.

If you contact us by email, we may receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your message. We use that information to respond to your request and to maintain the website.

Cookies and advertising

We may use Google AdSense and other third-party advertising technologies to display advertisements. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertisements, prevent repeated ads and support advertising functionality.

Google states that publishers using AdSense must clearly disclose the use of cookies in their privacy policy. Google also states that its advertising cookies can be used to serve ads based on a user's visits to this site or other sites, depending on settings and applicable policies.

Users can manage Google's personalized advertising preferences through Google Ads Settings.

Third-party services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. Those websites have their own privacy practices and policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, that service may process information as described in its own policies and applicable terms.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for parents, caregivers, educators and other adults seeking information. It is not designed to collect personal information directly from children.

Please do not submit a child's sensitive personal information through public comments, email or any form on this website unless it is necessary and appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, services or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Important: This page is a general website privacy notice, not legal advice. You should review it for your actual data collection, advertising, analytics, contact forms and applicable legal requirements before publishing.
